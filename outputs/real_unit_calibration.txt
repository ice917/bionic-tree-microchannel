thickness sensitivity: R_th and dP both scale as 1/t, so the
numbers above move by the same factor if the slab thickness
assumption moves. The *ratios* tree-vs-control do not.

microchannel reference: Ecotherm production data gives fin
spacing P <= 0.5 mm and Dh = 2*P*L/(P+L) ~= 2*P (L/P > 15),
so Dh = 0.4-1.0 mm for P = 0.2-0.5 mm. The model's capillaries
are r0=0.5 mm (D=1.0 mm): the r^4 penalty is 1.0-39.1x, a
sensitivity range, not the historical 16-100x (16 needs the
interior P=0.25 mm; 100 needs P=0.158 mm, outside the range).
