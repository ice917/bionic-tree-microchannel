                   -> model is 0.5x to 0.2x worse
  flow 2.01 L/min  ->  tree dP =  17.77 kPa, control dP = 0.03579 kPa  (497x)
                   published cold plate dP = 9 kPa at 1 kW (tree is 1.97x at 700 W)

thickness sensitivity: R_th and dP both scale as 1/t, so the
numbers above move by the same factor if the slab thickness
assumption moves. The *ratios* tree-vs-control do not.

microchannel reference: production cold plates use 0.2-0.5 mm
channels; this model's capillaries are r0=0.5 mm (1.0 mm
diameter), i.e. 2-5x wider than the
narrowest production channels. The r^4 penalty is therefore
not an artefact of the toy scale.
