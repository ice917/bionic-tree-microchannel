ELECTRICAL ANALOGUE OF THE POISEUILLE NETWORK
  pressure p  <->  node voltage v
  flux Q      <->  branch current i
  R = L/D     <->  resistance (D = 1  =>  R = L)
  supply b    <->  injected current source
  so the graph solve L p = b is solved natively by Simscape.
